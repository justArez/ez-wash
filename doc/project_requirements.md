# Smart Automated Car Wash Management System

## Topic Title
* **English (TA):** Smart Automated Car Wash Management System with Advance Booking & Loyalty Program

## Context & Overview
* Integrates AI and CRM technologies into an automated wash management system to boost customer retention (up to 45%).
* Focuses on digital tracking of points/history, tiered customer benefits (Silver/Gold/Platinum), and personalized rewards.

## Primary Actors
* **Customers:** Vehicle owners seeking convenient booking and rewards.
* **Admin:** Wash station operators managing daily operations, analyzing performance, and configuring promotions.

## Functional Requirements

### Loyalty Engine
* Track points, spend, and visit counts.
* Auto-upgrade or downgrade tiers during monthly reviews.
* Points redemption for discounts, free washes, or add-ons.
* Points expiration setting (expires after 12 months).

### Customer Features
* Account linking via license plate and phone number.
* View point balances and wash history.
* Tier-based advance booking windows (Member: 7 days, Silver: 10 days, Gold: 12 days, Platinum: 14 days) with priority queue access.
* Auto-apply tier perks at checkout.

### Admin Features
* Configure tier rules, point rates, perks, and targeted promotions (e.g., target "Silver+" members only).
* *Note: Excludes online payment implementation and refund management.*

## Main Entities
* Customer
* Customer's Vehicles
* Customer's point
* Booking / Wash history
* System's Promotion
